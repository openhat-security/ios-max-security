Level 3 · does not erase

Self-hosted MDM

You can enroll an iPhone in MDM without wiping it. You run the server. OpenHat does not manage the phone and cannot see your data.

You manage this. We do not.

Level 3 is optional. OpenHat does not operate an MDM for you, cannot see photos, messages, or settings, and has no access to your device. If you enroll, the server is yours.

Apple allows user-approved MDM enrollment without erasing the iPhone. That path can push Level 1 / Level 2 profiles later. It cannot hide Instagram or Snapchat. Those app hides need Level 4 (erase + Supervise).
