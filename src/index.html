Maximize your privacy on iOS

Developed by Adam Siwiec

Below, you'll find profiles that disable all of the invasive features from Apple which I've found. Level 1 is the most basic privacy profile, which disables the most invasive features from Apple, such as Apple Intelligence, Location Services, 'Analytics and Improvements', Tracking, along with additional sensitive PII collected by Apple which is surely not sold to third parties (or the gov't 🙄). Read more

I created these profiles while doing a deep dive into how Apple and its third parties track data. It started to really bother me when I had a conversation with someone, and suddenly got ads about what we discussed—without even using a mobile device. How do they know? There's no way Siri could be listening, right? Isn't that illegal? Turns out, it's not. But it should be.
Frankly, that's not even the most serious privacy or security issue I found during my research. But when companies donate enough money to the gov't, the rules don't apply to them. On the bright side, there are ways to block much of the invasive features—though only up to a point.
Let's be real: who is going to keep up with every single fucking update and disable every new invasive feature from Apple? Personally, after turning off everything I can, I hold off on updating until absolutely necessary. I trust vulnerabilities in previous iOS versions more than I trust Apple's new releases these days.
