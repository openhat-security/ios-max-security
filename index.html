Can a link show what is currently on the iPhone?

No live readout

Safari, a PWA, and this page are sandboxed. They cannot open Location Services, Analytics, Lockdown Mode, or app permissions and report back. Shortcuts cannot read those toggles either.

Where the live state actually lives (tap these on the phone):

  1. Settings → Privacy & Security — tracking, analytics, ads, location, App Privacy Report (which servers apps contact).
  2. Settings → General → VPN & Device Management — installed profiles and DNS. After install, open OpenHat Max Privacy to see payloads.
  3. Greyed-out toggles in Settings mean the profile already owns that switch. If you can still flip it, it is a leftover tap below.

Bookmark this page (#preview / #audit) or use the Privacy Audit home-screen icon. Checkboxes you tick are stored in this browser only — that is how you record “already done,” because the phone will not tell us.
